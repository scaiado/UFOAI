## 3. Four independent lines converge on mechanical abrasion

| source | date | wording |
|---|---|---|
| Gendarmerie telex | 9 Jan 1981 | « traces de ripage de pneumatiques » |
| Witness, quoted in NT-16 §3 | — | « sur la courbe du cercle existent des genres de **traces de ripage** » |
| NT-16 §4.2 | 1983 | « la surface … semblait avoir été **frottée** sur une faible distance » ; « cette terre a subi un **frottement** en surface » |
| SNEAP | 1981 | « Le **frottement** constaté et observé a eu … » |
| LDP | 1981 | residue « pouvant provenir d'une **peinture noire primaire à base de Carbon black** » |
| NT-16 §8 | 1983 | « interpré]tations possibles (**choc, frottement**…) restent cependant trop … » |

Geometry agrees across sources: two arcs of circles, witness calls it
« plutôt un fer à cheval » (rather a horseshoe); ring ~10 cm wide; **80 cm
long** in both the telex and the gendarmerie sketch reproduced as `CROQUIS 7`
(0.80 m × 0.10 m, the same as the telex's 10 cm width; an earlier "14 cm" here was an OCR error); earth strongly compacted
into a crust ~1 cm thick.

**This does not settle the case and is not presented as doing so.** GEPAN
considered and did not adopt a vehicle explanation, and the vegetation chemistry
is real and unexplained by abrasion alone. The point is narrower: a prosaic
mechanism is documented in the primary record from day one, by the gendarmes
who made the measurements, and the project should carry that forward.

## 4. The pigment data — and why the usual framing is wrong

NT-16 §7, backed by the newly-readable 60-page INRA series. Samples from the
trace periphery (d ≈ 1.5 m) against **controls at 20 m**, split young vs aged
leaves, so leaf-age and distance effects are separated. Trace still visible at
**40 days** (17.02.81), helped by low rainfall and the plot being unfrequented.

Verbatim: « affaibli de **30 à 50 %** en formes actives et enrichi en formes
inactives ».

| pigment | observed | γ at 10⁶ rads | ratio | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| chlorophyll A | −33% | −30% | 1.10 | comparable |
| chlorophyll B | −28% | −46% | **0.61** | observed **milder** |
| phéophytine | −31% | — | — | — |
| β-carotene | **−50 to −57%** | −20% | **2.67** | observed worse |
| violaxanthine | **−80%** (young leaves) | −40% | **2.00** | observed worse |

**The common framing — "damage exceeding a megarad of gamma" — is wrong.**
Chlorophylls are comparable to or *milder* than the γ benchmark; only the
carotenoids exceed it, by 2.0–2.7×. NT-16 notes the three chlorophyll-family
figures « montrent une grande homogénéité » (−33, −28, −31%) while β-carotene
and violaxanthine break away.

So INRA's argument is about the **pattern**, not the magnitude: carotenoids are
disproportionately destroyed relative to chlorophylls, which is not what
ionising radiation does. Hence, verbatim:

> « L'action du rayonnement nucléaire ne présente donc pas d'analogie avec celle
> de la source énergétique impliquée dans le phénomène observé. »

and the proposed alternative:

> « une intensification spécifique de la transformation des chlorophylles en
> phéophytines et des chlorophyllides en phéophorbides (**par perte de Mg⁺⁺**)
> pourrait être liée à l'action d'**un champ énergétique de type électrique**. »

**Consequence for §6.** The untested fallout-fogging alternative to
arXiv:2604.00056 is discriminated here not by total damage but by the
**carotenoid-to-chlorophyll ratio**. Any irradiation or fogging mechanism should
hit chlorophylls at least as hard as carotenoids. At Trans-en-Provence the
ratio is inverted. That is the measurement to apply, and it is sharper than the
benchmark §1.2 recorded.

## 5. GEPAN's own disposition

> « l'absence de preuve n'étant pas, là non plus, la preuve de l'absence, ceci
> ne suffit pas à certifier la véracité … »

GEPAN declines to treat the physical findings as confirming the witness, and
brackets the observation distance explicitly rather than asserting it:

> « Probablement Monsieur C n'était jamais à plus de 70 mètres du phénomène,
> mais n'en a été à aucun moment à moins d'une trentaine de mètres »
> → **30 m < distance < 70 m**

**A near-miss worth recording.** The telex says ~50 m and NT-16's synthesis says
~30 m, which looks like a 40% drift between the contemporaneous police report
and the analysis two years later — a provenance-drift finding of exactly the
kind §6 collects. **It is not one.** GEPAN's own reconstruction brackets 30–70 m,
which contains both figures, and notes the observation was made « dans un cadre
de référence bien connu du témoin, ce qui diminue les causes d'erreurs ». No
drift. Found only by checking both ranges instead of confirming the discrepancy.

## 6. Trans-en-Provence is n = 1

Scanning all 3,404 GEIPAN cases by **document filename** (not keyword), 20 carry
an "analysis" document — but reading the filenames shows most are `Analyse
video`, `Analyse photo`, `Expertise cassette audio`, even `Analyse recit`:
image, audio and narrative forensics. Genuine physical-sample analyses: **five**.

**In the only national archive that separates "unidentified for lack of
information" (C) from "unidentified despite adequate information" (D),
Trans-en-Provence is the sole class-D case with a physical-sample laboratory
analysis.** The other four sample-bearing cases were identified or probably
identified.

The physical-trace evidence class, against ground truth, has **one** unexplained
member with lab work. Any argument that rests on "the physical trace cases"
as a category is resting on n = 1 plus four resolved controls.

### The point that cuts the other way

GEIPAN applied **comparable or greater** analytical effort to the cases it
resolved:

| case | lab pages |
|---|---|
| SAINT-SULPICE (A) | **127** (APAVE 96 + CREA 31) |
| MAISONNAIS (A) | **56** |
| BARBEZIEUX (B) | 60-page report + 2-page soil analysis |
| **TRANS-EN-PROVENCE (D)** | **127** (NT-16 67 + INRA series 60) |

So TEP's D classification is **not** an artefact of thin investigation. It
received as much laboratory work as the cases that were solved, and remained
unsolved. That is a fair point in its favour and is recorded as such.

### An unforced parallel, flagged as suggestive only

The TEP telex records that the witness's attention was drawn by « un léger
sifflement » (a light whistling). Saint-Sulpice is described as « chute d'un
objet précédée par un sifflement » — and there the whistle was a falling
Nebelwerfer rocket component. Suggestive, not probative; recorded because a
matched control set is exactly where such parallels should be looked for.

### Honest limitation

**Barbezieux's soil analysis cannot be read.** `Analyse echantillons sol-R1.pdf`
is 2 pages of handwriting; OCR returned 786 characters of noise. The class-B
soil comparator — the nearest thing to a direct control on TEP's soil work — is
therefore unavailable without manual transcription. Reported as unreadable
rather than interpreted.
