# The 37 AAWSAP DIRDs — what they actually are

`omega/dird_analysis.py` · `evidence_vault/01_PURSUE_PRIMARY/dird_analysis.json`

The DIRDs were the strongest remaining "positive" in this project: 37
government-commissioned Defense Intelligence Reference Documents on wormholes,
warp drive, antigravity, negative mass, quantum-vacuum energy extraction,
metamaterials, invisibility cloaking. They had only ever been **grepped**.

One question decided their interpretation: **do they cite observed UAP
performance as motivation or design requirement, or are they open-literature
reviews built from published physics?**

## The answer

| measurement | result |
|---|---|
| DIRDs mentioning UAP / UFO / anomalous aerial **at all** | **1 of 37** |
| DIRDs citing observed performance as a design driver | 10 of 37 (mostly generic engineering usage) |
| open-literature citation markers | **1,541** |
| closed / internal citation markers | **56** |
| **ratio open : closed** | **27.5 : 1** |
| feasibility language | infeasible-now 34 · far-future 7 · near-term 42 |

**36 of 37 never mention the phenomenon.** They are literature surveys built
from *Physical Review*, *Nature*, arXiv, AIAA and textbooks. D139's own section
IV is titled *"Constructing a Traversable Wormhole is not Easy"*.

Each carries the same boilerplate: *"This product is one in a series of advanced
technology reports produced in FY 2009 under the Defense Intelligence Agency,
Defense Warning Office's Advanced Aerospace Weapon System Applications
(AAWSAP) Program"*, prepared by **Acquisition Support Division (DWO-3),
Defense Warning Office, Directorate for Analysis, DIA**.

**So "the government funded research into NHI-level technology" deflates to "a
threat-assessment office paid contractors to survey the open journals."** That
is what such an office does whether or not anything has been observed. The
DIRDs are evidence of *programme existence and scope*, not of capability,
recovery, or contact.

Authorship is anonymised as **"AAP Person N"** — 31 distinct IDs across the 37,
occupying a near-contiguous block (1, 57, 58, 63–90). The AAWSAP personnel
numbering therefore ran to at least 90.

## The single exception: D128

`DOW-UAP-D128 — Anomalous Acute and Subacute Field Effects on Human Biological
Tissues`, 11 March 2010, ICOD 1 December 2009, 91,892 chars. Author *AAP Person 8*.

It is structurally unlike the other 36:

- **14 UAP references** (the rest: zero)
- **14 closed/internal citations — the highest of any DIRD**, against only 15 open
- **Appendix A is the "Schuessler Catalog of UFO-Related Human Physiological
  Effects"** — it ingests a UFO case catalogue as a data appendix
- cites Sturrock, *Physical evidence related to UFO reports: Proceedings of a
  workshop at Pocantico Conference Center*, 1998, *J. Sci. Exploration* 22

### What it concludes — verbatim

> *"In summary, the evidence is solid that the military and aerospace
> industries' own classified, proprietary and unclassified literature, as well
> as the certain evidence of many aerospace RF-related microwave,
> antenna-related, high power (HP) microwave, infrasonic/sonic, and thermal
> (from any source) energy deposition accidents … is **congruent with the
> anomalous vehicle reports**. This means that one can not discount the claimed
> and often observed injuries as being **real-world (although current and
> likely advanced beyond public information) technologies**, and which are
> causes of these effects and injuries."*

**The only AAWSAP DIRD that examines actual UAP case data concludes the injury
patterns map onto known terrestrial RF/microwave/thermal energy-deposition
accidents** — explicitly including classified terrestrial technology. That is a
prosaic finding, reached inside the programme, on the programme's own data.

### Its one exotic alternative — and it is falsifiable

> *"To give but one hypothesis with regard to exotic mechanisms, there is the
> possibility that those are effects predicted by General Relativity Theory …
> in which the **blackbody heat spectrum of an object (an anomalous craft)
> would be blue-shifted** (increased in frequency) under conditions of
> **spacetime manipulation for lift and propulsion**. Were a human exposed to
> blue-shifting at relatively close range, symptoms associated with broadband
> radiation — microwave, visible, UV, **soft X-rays** — could be expected."*

This is a real physical prediction with a signature: **spacetime-manipulation
propulsion implies ionising exposure at close range.**

## The cross-document test nobody has run — and why it does not resolve

D128 predicts an **ionising** signature. Trans-en-Provence is the only
physical-trace case in the French state archive with full laboratory analysis,
and INRA **tested and rejected an ionising mechanism** there, verbatim:

> *« L'action du rayonnement nucléaire ne présente donc pas d'analogie avec
> celle de la source énergétique impliquée dans le phénomène observé. »*

with the quantitative basis: carotenoids disproportionately destroyed
(β-carotene −50/−57%, violaxanthine −80%) while chlorophylls were **comparable
to or milder than** 10⁶ rads of γ (A −33% vs −30%, B −28% vs −46%).

**But the two cannot be set against each other, and the reason is itself the
finding.** The TEP gendarmerie telex records, of the witness at 30–70 m:
`AUCUN EFFET RESSENTI PAR LE TEMOIN` — **no effect felt**. TEP is a case with
vegetation damage and **no human effect**. D128's catalogue is of cases **with**
human effects, for which no laboratory trace analysis exists.

**The project's two best evidence bases do not overlap.** The one case with full
physical-trace chemistry has no human exposure; the one document with a human
exposure model has no trace chemistry. That is a structural gap in the evidence,
not a result, and no amount of re-analysis of either closes it.

## Provenance limits of D128, stated

Its supporting medical data is **unpublished**: *"(Green & Schuessler,
unpublished findings…)"*, and the source list includes **"Unpublished HIPPA
files"** and *"Unpublished Personal Archives"*, plus private communications
dated 1973, 1998, 1999, 2002, 2004, 2005. HIPAA-protected medical records
explain the `(b)(6)` privacy redactions, and they also mean the evidentiary base
is not independently checkable.

## Two new FOIA targets, named in the primary documents

1. **"Technical Study 10.0 Human Effects"** — an internal AAWSAP study cited in
   D128 (*"[Adapted from Technical Study 10.0 Human Effects]"*) and **not in the
   PURSUE release**. Cited by exactly one document; the only internal Technical
   Study referenced anywhere in the 37.
2. **The Schuessler Catalog** as held by AAWSAP — D128's Appendix A. The release
   contains the DIRD but the appendix content is not separately available.

## Disposition

- The DIRD corpus is **programme-scope evidence, not capability evidence**.
  27.5:1 open-to-closed citations and 1-in-37 phenomenon mentions settle it.
- **D128 is the exception and it cuts prosaic**: inside the programme, on the
  programme's own case data, the conclusion was terrestrial RF energy
  deposition including classified terrestrial systems.
- D128's exotic alternative is **falsifiable and unfalsified** — it predicts
  ionising exposure, and the one case with trace chemistry had no human
  exposure to test it against.
